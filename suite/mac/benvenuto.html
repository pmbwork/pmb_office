pmb_office @V_SUITE@

Installs the PMB programs you choose in the “Customize” screen into Applications:

  • pmb_docs @V_DOCS@ — text documents (.docx, .odt, .pdoc)
  • pmb_sheets @V_SHEETS@ — spreadsheets (.xlsx, .ods, .csv)

Free software under the GNU GPL v3 licence. Works offline: your documents stay on your Mac.
